# Monetization Strategy: The Peach Economy

## Overview
The "Peach" economy is designed to be accessible to nursing students and interns in Delta State, considering their likely budget constraints while ensuring the platform is sustainable.

## Currency: Pits
- **Base Cost:** ~N20 - N25 per Pit.
- **Ripen Action:** Costs 5 Pits (~N100 - N125).

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. The Student Saver (Micro-Transaction)
*Target: Students (Years 1-3) on a tight allowance.*
- **Price:** N500
- **Pits:** 20 Pits
- **Value:** 4 Ripens.
- **Why:** Low barrier to entry. Equivalent to a cheap snack or transport fare.

### 2. The Intern Bundle (Best Value)
*Target: Interns and working nurses.*
- **Price:** N1,000
- **Pits:** 50 Pits (Bonus: +10% extra value compared to base)
- **Value:** 10 Ripens.
- **Why:** Encourages bulk buying. "One k" is a standard psychological price point in Nigeria.

### 3. The "Double Shift" Boss (Premium)
*Target: Senior Nurses or Power Users.*
- **Price:** N2,500
- **Pits:** 150 Pits
- **Value:** 30 Ripens.
- **Why:** For users who want to unlock many matches without frequent refills.

## Payment Integration
- **Gateways:** Paystack or Flutterwave.
- **Methods:** USSD, Bank Transfer, and Card (USSD is crucial for student demographic).

## Free Pits Strategy (Growth)
- **Sign-up Bonus:** 25 Pits (5 Free Ripens) to hook users.
- **Referral Bonus:** "Bring a Nurse friend, get 10 Pits."
