# Monetization Strategy

## Pit Pricing Tiers (Sapele Student Budget Optimized)

We understand the student struggle. Pricing is designed to be affordable for nursing students and interns in Delta State.

| Tier Name | Pits | Price (NGN) | Cost per Pit | Value Proposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Student Saver** | 20 Pits | N500 | N25.00 | Enough for 4 Ripens. Perfect for a quick check. |
| **Intern Bundle** | 50 Pits | N1,000 | N20.00 | **Best Value!** 10 Ripens. For the serious socialite. |
| **Head Nurse Stash** | 120 Pits | N2,000 | N16.67 | Bulk discount. Boss level. |

## Feature Costs

- **Ripen Match:** 5 Pits (Reveals unblurred photo and real name).
- **Super Peach (Boost):** 10 Pits (Profile highlighted for 24 hours) - *Future Feature*.
